# References

Study the best work for the same kind of surface and the same audience before choosing a direction. Borrow structure, proportion and behavior. Never borrow text, logos, illustrations, photos or a whole layout.

## What to extract

Give `ult:design-scout` the surface, the audience and three to five URLs. Ask for numbers:

- **Layout**: max content width, column grid, section padding, what is above the fold at 1440 and at 390.
- **Type**: families, the size scale, weights, tracking on display sizes, measure.
- **Color**: surface, text and accent values; how many hues; where the accent appears and where it does not.
- **Density**: base spacing unit, row height in tables and lists, how much fits on one screen.
- **Motion**: what moves on load, on hover, on scroll; durations and easings; what stays still.
- **One transferable idea** per site.

## How to use what comes back

1. Note what every reference shares: that is the convention for this surface, and breaking it needs a reason.
2. Note where they differ: that is where your direction can be distinctive.
3. Write in `DESIGN.md` what you borrow, from where and why, in one line each.
4. After building, put your screenshot beside the strongest reference. Name the three largest gaps (usually spacing rhythm, type scale and restraint) and close the biggest.

A reference informs a decision. If your result could be mistaken for the reference, you copied; change the direction.
